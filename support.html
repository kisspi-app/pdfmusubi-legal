02FAQ

How do I remove ads?
Purchase Premium (in-app purchase) and ads are no longer shown.
Premium disappeared after changing devices
Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase, then tap "Restore Purchase" in the app. It is restored at no extra cost.
Are my files sent anywhere?
No. Merging, splitting, compressing, editing pages, converting photos to PDF, and scanning documents are done on your device.
